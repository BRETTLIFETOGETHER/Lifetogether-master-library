# Shared product experience across ten ministry sites

The ten standalone sites use one generated product layer so source corrections and experience improvements remain synchronized.

Each site now includes:

- Source-backed discovery across its ministry-specific catalog
- Title, subtitle, stated duration, weekly sessions, and devotional-day outlines when the source connects them
- A 10-, 21-, 30-, or 40-day journey configurator with four- or six-session rhythms
- Audience editions for adults, leaders, families, youth, students, advisors, and workplace teams
- A complete production manifest covering teaching, groups, daily devotionals, family, youth, student, leader, campaign operations, digital layout, and print files
- A profile with formation context and selectable intelligence frameworks
- Single-campaign and all-access product pathways ready for approved Shopify product URLs
- A portable production brief and share action
- A Lulu print-studio handoff for physical curriculum
- An advisor-referral brief that does not fabricate a public advisor directory
- Direct movement between the ministry site, the other nine sites, the master library, source history, and backend artifacts

The interface distinguishes source-connected material from work that still needs production. It does not treat a title-only catalog record as a finished manuscript or represent checkout as active until approved Shopify URLs and pricing are supplied.
