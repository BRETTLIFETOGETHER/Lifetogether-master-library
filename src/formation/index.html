03 / THE FLAGSHIP BOOK

Doing Church
Together

A Practical Guide to Aligning Your Church, Equipping Your People, and Reaching Your Community

Proposed 21-chapter outline

Explore the chapter direction. Full manuscripts are in development.

Download the outline ↗
01We Have to Think DifferentlyBegin with the people we are called to become together+

Establish the personal and pastoral reason for the book; introduce Brett’s story only through his approved account

02A Family to Belong ToMake room for people who need connection, care, and a place to contribute+

Describe belonging through concrete relationships, including people who arrive alone

03The Whole Church, All Week LongConnect the gathering with homes, groups, work, and community+

Introduce churchwide formation and the five formation environments

04Follow the Shepherd TogetherLead under Christ with conviction, humility, and accountability+

Clarify the relationship between Christ’s authority, pastoral leadership, and shared participation

05Bring the Vision into the RoomTurn stated values into the decisions people make+

Show how vision shapes team meetings, calendars, volunteer expectations, and ministry practices

06Give Every Leader a PartHelp many voices express a shared purpose+

Introduce collaborative planning, ministry-leader teaching, and appropriate responsibility

07Let Sunday Begin the ConversationPrepare every message for its life throughout the week+

Develop the sermon-to-conversation workflow and the relationship between planning and actual delivered teaching

08Give People Something to PracticeJoin daily devotion with reflection, action, and response+

Define short daily resources, realistic practices, and optional 90-second companion videos

09Put the Journey in Their HandsMake print and digital participation work together+

Design devotional journals, writable workbooks, accessible digital resources, and practical production schedules

10Build a Place to BelongHelp groups welcome people, care for one another, and continue+

Develop the group experience beyond launch, including hosts, shared responsibility, and the next study

11Bring Formation HomeEquip families for conversations already within reach+

Build Family Conversations for preschool, elementary, and youth while recognizing different household circumstances

12Let the Generations Build TogetherInvite young people and older adults into meaningful ministry+

Include student leadership, grandparents, mentoring, and opportunities to serve across generations

13Help People Find Their ContributionConnect gifts and willingness with supported service+

Develop volunteer invitation, discernment, training, and care

14Develop the Next LeaderMake apprenticeship part of the ministry you already lead+

Show how hosts, team leaders, coaches, and future pastors gain responsibility with support

15Join Your Neighbors’ LivesLet the church’s shared purpose reach into its community+

Connect listening, practical service, relationships, and gospel witness

16Make Room for the Work That MattersShare care, coordination, and preparation across an equipped team+

Address pastoral capacity, family time, care coverage, and systems that support human responsibility

17Put Resources Behind the CallingConnect stewardship, generosity, and legacy with responsible ministry+

Address formation, budgets, faithful funding, and meaningful participation by families with resources

18Learn Beyond Your Own ExperienceBring evaluated wisdom and useful tools into local ministry+

Explain approved source libraries, peer learning, publishing, production, and accountable AI assistance

19Bring the Church Together for a SeasonDesign a focused journey with shared teaching and practical action+

Apply campaign learning to purposeful 21-, 30-, and 40-day experiences without promising guaranteed transformation

20Plan for the Day AfterHelp relationships, practices, and new leaders continue+

Build the next group experience, ministry pathway, testimony review, and staff learning into the original plan

21Leave Something Others Can CarryEntrust the people, practices, and resources that make the work durable+

Translate the five-year horizon into successor development and a transferable ministry approach
