# The 21-Day Churchwide Intelligence Challenge

LifeTogether · Proposed outline · September 2026

## 1. The Ministry Intelligence Foundation

Connect what your church knows with how your people are formed

A shared formation objective, initial scope, and named owner

## 2. Church Intelligence

Understand your congregation, convictions, community, and calling

A one-page church profile and list of knowledge gaps

## 3. Pastor Intelligence

Preserve pastoral convictions while equipping others to lead

A teaching and voice brief with review responsibilities

## 4. Christian Life Intelligence

Connect teaching with the lives people actually lead

Priority life situations and appropriate next-step pathways

## 5. Vision and Values Intelligence

Make the church’s direction visible in ordinary ministry

Three values translated into observable practices

## 6. Sermon Intelligence

Carry the message into daily reflection, conversation, and action

A source-linked plan for one week of follow-up

## 7. Weekend Intelligence

Connect worship gatherings with the rest of the week

A coordinated gathering and follow-up plan

## 8. Devotional and Conversation Intelligence

Give every day a question worth discussing and a response worth trying

A daily-resource template with print and digital response space

## 9. Small Group Intelligence

Help people connect, participate, care, and develop as leaders

A group launch or strengthening plan with a continuation step

## 10. Sunday School and ABF Intelligence

Connect classroom learning with shared church priorities

A class application and alignment plan respecting its learning sequence

## 11. Family Intelligence

Equip households at the table, in the car, and at bedtime

A weekly Family Conversations plan with varied household pathways

## 12. Next Generation Intelligence

Adapt faithfully for preschool, elementary, and students

Age-specific objectives and a parent communication plan

## 13. Intergenerational and Legacy Intelligence

Help generations share faith, responsibility, and meaningful work

One partnership between generations and a leadership opportunity

## 14. Ministry and Volunteer Intelligence

Discover gifts and connect people with supported opportunities to serve

A volunteer pathway from invitation through coaching

## 15. Leadership Intelligence

Develop hosts, apprentices, coaches, and future ministry leaders

A development pathway with named mentors and next steps

## 16. Pastoral Care Intelligence

Coordinate personal care through an equipped and accountable team

A care referral and follow-up map

## 17. Mission and Community Intelligence

Learn your community and create appropriate opportunities to participate

A community listening step and one practical service or invitation pathway

## 18. Stewardship and Generosity Intelligence

Connect resources, discipleship, ministry, and responsible planning

A stewardship formation objective and a clear ministry resource plan

## 19. Campaign Intelligence

Launch a focused experience with Day 41 already in view

A campaign objective, scope, calendar, and continuation plan

## 20. Production and Publishing Intelligence

Prepare useful print, video, and digital resources from reviewed sources

An asset list, production calendar, and review process

## 21. Learning and Multiplication Intelligence

Listen to responses and decide what to strengthen, repeat, and entrust

A proposed 90-day pilot with evidence measures and an owner
