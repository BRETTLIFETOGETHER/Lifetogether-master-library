# Churchwide Formation — Starter Guide

LifeTogether · A planning tool for pastors and ministry teams

## Begin with people

What do you hope people will understand, practice, and experience together after the next series?

## Plan one week

- Sunday: Receive the message and name a response.
- Monday: Recall its central biblical truth.
- Tuesday: Reflect honestly on a difficulty or question.
- Wednesday: Talk with someone about the teaching.
- Thursday: Try one practical response.
- Friday: Consider an act of service or invitation.
- Saturday: Reflect on what happened and what remains unfinished.

## Connect the settings

Prepare a daily reflection, an adult group conversation, family prompts for preschool/elementary/students, and an appropriate ministry application. Represent the teaching children and youth actually heard accurately. Include a route for people without a participating household.

## Share the leadership

Invite a ministry leader to record an optional 90-second companion: reconnect, illustrate, ask an open question, and invite a response. Use real stories with permission.

## Before a campaign

Choose a focused objective, prepare leaders, review resources, plan print delivery, and decide what groups and families will do afterward. A 21-, 30-, or 40-day format supports the objective; its duration does not guarantee transformation.

These prompts are a planning template, not a completed sermon-specific devotional or validated assessment.
